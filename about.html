About the project

The site was built for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology. It turns the Australian Government's Energy Rating data into a story for households choosing a new TV: how star ratings, screen size and technology affect the yearly running cost.

The Home page gives context on appliance energy use in Australia, and the Televisions page presents the data and explains how to read the visualisation.

About me

I'm Hoang Trong Toan, a student at Swinburne University of Technology studying AI.

About the data

The figures come from the Energy Rating dataset published on data.gov.au under a Creative Commons Attribution licence. It lists TV models registered by suppliers, with their star rating and labelled energy use in kWh per year. The numbers reflect standardised testing, so your own use and electricity tariff will differ.
