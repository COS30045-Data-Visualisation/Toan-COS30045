Charts built with D3

A set of charts drawn from the Energy Rating data using D3, each with labelled axes.

Which brands register the most TVs?

Number of registered TV models for the 15 biggest brands (horizontal bar chart).

Which screen technology uses the most energy?

Mean annual energy use of 55 inch TVs by screen technology. Screens are only comparable at the same size, so one size is used here.

How does screen size change energy use?

Mean annual energy use for every screen size from 24 to 98 inches. Each dot is a size and the line joins them.

How are TV models split by size?

Share of registered models in the small, medium and large categories.
