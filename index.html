Appliance energy use in Australian homes

Australian households spend a large share of their energy bill on heating, cooling, hot water and everyday appliances. Knowing which appliances use the most power helps you cut costs.

Where the energy goes

Appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines and televisions run for years, so a small difference in yearly energy use adds up. This project looks closely at televisions, using the Australian Government's Energy Rating data.

Energy rating labels help buyers compare models. More stars means lower running costs, but screen size matters too, so compare stars between TVs of the same size.

Frequently asked questions

More stars means the TV uses less energy for its screen size. The label also shows the estimated kWh used per year, assuming 10 hours of use a day.

Multiply the labelled kWh per year by your tariff. At 28.55 cents per kWh, the median TV in the data (397 kWh a year) costs about $113 a year.

The Explore page has an interactive histogram and scatterplot that you can filter by screen technology.
